03 · DECIDE BEFORE CODE

Open decisions block code

Third, and the most important: decide before code. A real one from Contami: I sent the agent a screenshot of the bank list on my phone and said “each transaction needs its own page”. It came back with a spec, a clickable mock at three widths, competitor research, and twelve decisions, each with options and its recommendation. Four are already decided, like the route. Eight are open, like this one: on a wide screen, list and page side by side, option A. Until those close, the page doesn’t get built. The small fixes it found along the way shipped the same day.
