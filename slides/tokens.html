And then the bottleneck moved. In week one, agents merged 825 PRs into Contami, and I was the bottleneck. So on the 18th the lead started asking me only for decisions. Week two dropped to 216. Not because of me this time: I had one Claude subscription, and the tokens ran out first. So on the 20th the lead started picking the model per task: a fast one for lookups, Opus for building and review, Fable only when I approve. The lesson: the bottleneck always moves, and the setup has to move with it.
